For the SAARTHI digital twin. OpenStreetMap, which the twin's road network comes from, marks a signal at 3 of these 27 junctions. Mark each one as it is on the ground.

How to check: open Street View for the junction and look for signal heads (the light poles) on any arm. If the imagery is old or unclear, choose Can't tell and add a note; a site visit or the traffic police's list settles it.

Junctions on the ambulance route in the twin are listed first. Only roads in the model (tertiary and above) are listed: if a signal is where a smaller sector road meets a main road, write it in the note on the nearest row.
