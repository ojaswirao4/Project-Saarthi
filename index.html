01 · The problem

A 15-minute stretch that steals an hour, every day.

The real damage comes from unpredictability and gridlock rather than raw volume. Five things pile up at once, and fixed timers can't break the cascade.

Merging flows

Flyover, ramp, office and mall traffic all converge on one corridor.

Single-point bottlenecks

One blocked junction or narrow road backs up the whole loop.

Kerbside blockage

Illegal parking, cabs and event vehicles eat the capacity.

Fixed signal timing

Can't adapt to tidal peaks or a sudden incident. Each signal runs its own clock.

Peak concentration

Offices, studios, malls and metro all peak in the same window.

The insight

Noida already owns the hardware: the ITMS command centre in Sector 94, ~82 CCTV feeds, ~40 adaptive junctions and ANPR. Today it mostly watches and fines. SAARTHI turns the same cameras into a brain, with no rip-and-replace.

02 · The approach

Treat the corridor as one graph, not a string of signals.

Junctions become nodes, roads become edges, and congestion becomes a signal flowing across the graph. That lets one model see a queue forming two junctions away and act before it arrives.

Nodes

Junctions

Every real intersection on the corridor. Each node carries its live queue in PCU, its current signal phase and its spillback risk.

Edges

Roads

Each edge carries occupancy, mean speed and the platoons travelling along it toward the next node.

Model

Spatio-temporal GNN

Forecasts where the next jam forms, 15–60 min ahead. The control brain then coordinates every node against one shared corridor objective.

The closed loop

Sense → Predict → Decide & act → Enforce → Shape demand. Every stage runs in the twin.

03 · The live twin

Same traffic. Two control systems. Watch them diverge.

Both twins get identical arrivals, with the same vehicles at the same seconds. Only the control system differs. Inject an incident and it hits both at the same instant, and the stopwatch times how long each takes to recover. Switch the area to the real Film City corridor, Connaught Place or Sector 62 to run on the actual road network pulled from OpenStreetMap: every junction, U-turn, slip road and flyover.

    04 · Plan ahead
    
      
    Know a disruption is coming? Rehearse it in the twin first.
    
      
    Enter a protest, a VIP movement or a big event before it happens. The planner simulates today's approach and several SAARTHI plans on identical traffic, then recommends one with a timeline, public diversions and a separate VIP route. It can also add an unplanned crash to check that the plan gets caught and corrected.

        06 · Architecture
        
      
        Six cooperating layers, each independently upgradeable.
        
      
        A software and edge-compute layer that rides on cameras and controllers Noida largely already owns.
        
    
        
    
        
      
        1
        Sensing
        Reused CCTV/ANPR + new edge cameras, floating-car data, optional radar.
        field
        
      
        2
        Edge perception
        YOLOv11 detection + BoT-SORT tracking + PCU density on Jetson Orin. <20 ms per frame, only KB/s of metadata leaves the pole.
        simulated here
        
      
        3
        Data & digital twin
        Streaming pipeline, calibrated twin, spatio-temporal forecasting. Every plan is proven here before it touches a real signal.
        running on this page
        
      
        4
        Control brain
        Multi-agent RL (QMIX / MAPPO) on a deterministic safety floor. Min/max green, pedestrian clearance, anti-starvation and command-centre override can never be violated.
        running on this page
        
      
        5
        Actuation
        Adaptive signals via NTCIP, emergency green corridor, dynamic lanes / VMS, ramp metering at the DND merge.
        simulated here
        
      
        6
        Integration & ops
        Sector 94 command centre, dashboards, MLOps, alerts to police / 112.
        field
